Use free AI voice (Kokoro)

Open-source Kokoro voice, English only. The first use downloads about 90 MB and then sets itself up — that first run can take a minute or two, and it gets faster afterwards. It then works offline and costs nothing. On slower phones the OpenAI voice will always feel snappier.

AI voice — natural, keeps playing in the background
Use AI voice (OpenAI)

Get a key at platform.openai.com under “API keys”. It stays in this browser and is sent only to OpenAI. Usage is billed to your API account. With AI voice on, listening continues when you minimize the app or lock the screen.

Sync & Share with Claude
Sync my articles across devices

Turn this on on each device and they all show one library: paste an article on your computer and it appears on your phone (tap ↻ or reopen the app), with your place in each article and the played checkmarks carried over. It also keeps a cloud copy of your recent articles (text only — never your keys or settings) that Claude can read: paste the Claude link into a Claude chat and ask away. Anyone with the link can read these articles; turning this off stops this device from syncing.
